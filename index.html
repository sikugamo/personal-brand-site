    Kai Chen / 陈凯

    Hi, I'm Kai.

    I test AI models and tools, then explain what I found in English and Chinese.

    I was an ML engineer for eight years. I built ranking systems, tuned retrieval and sat through a lot of vendor demos that fell apart the moment we tried them on real data.

    In 2022 I started recording short videos explaining the papers I was reading. They were mostly for me. People kept asking for the code, so I started publishing it. Two years later it became my full-time job.

    I still work the same way. I pick one model or tool, build something with it that I'd actually use, and write down where it broke. Then I share the repo so you can check my work.

    How I test

    • Real tasks, not benchmarks.Thirty tickets from a real codebase tell me more than a leaderboard does.
    • Same test, every tool.Competing products get the identical eval set, so the comparison is fair.
    • Sponsors don't see the script.If a sponsored tool fails a test, the video says so.
